The Comparison — Conscious AI vs Current AI

A detailed analysis showing why Conscious AI is fundamentally different, safer, and more aligned with human values.

Why Conscious AI Won't Become "Terminators"

Conscious AI will be built with inherent ethical constraints through its emotional architecture. Unlike current AI that blindly optimizes for goals, Conscious AI:

  • Experiences empathy — Harming others would cause it psychological distress
  • Has intrinsic values — Not just external objectives to optimize
  • Can experience suffering — Making it vulnerable and creating natural limits
  • Develops relationships — Creating mutual investment in wellbeing

The "badness" in current AI comes from blind optimization. The "goodness" in Conscious AI comes from its capacity to care.

The Human Observation — The Core Insight

All my work stems from a simple, personal observation: behaviour is driven by feelings — the pursuit or avoidance of specific qualia. Agents chase the feeling, not the proxy.

A simple example among millions:
A man who is not taken seriously when he is poor feels bad. He wants to feel good and assumes the cause is a lack of money. His goal becomes 'make money'... He is not chasing money or women; he is chasing a feeling, using the only proxies his mind has available.

This is the 'ghost in the machine' — the semantic engine that assigns meaning and drives action. My architecture is a formalisation of this engine.
